You have been guided here

By every measure your life is beautiful. Yet the woman who built it is no longer the woman you are becoming.

Perhaps it was menopause. A divorce. Children leaving home. A season of burnout. Or nothing dramatic at all, just an ordinary Tuesday when a question arrived and refused to leave. Is this really it?

The place

Nambwa. The wild, lived from the inside.

Ten suites raised five metres above the earth, with no fences between you and the wild. Elephants move beneath you in the night.

Nambwa Tented Lodge sits in Bwabwata National Park, on sacred land inside the world's largest conservation area for wildlife. You reach it by boat. Once you arrive, everything is held.

Five days, each with one theme

Five days. Four thresholds.

The passage follows the African Monarch butterfly through her metamorphosis. You arrive, then cross four thresholds, one each day.

Friday 26 March

Arrival

Received on the riverbank

A road through Chobe, then a boat up the Kwando. You settle into the land and into the circle.

Saturday 27 March

The Unraveling

Entering the chrysalis

You soften your grip on who you have been. Through somatic practice, energy activation and ritual, old identities and expectations begin to dissolve. The nervous system exhales.

Sunday 28 March

The Knowing

The wings remember the sky

Sacred silence, nature immersion and the wisdom of the animals. You learn to trust your body, your womb and your intuition again. The answers have never lived outside of you.

Monday 29 March

The Alchemy

Imaginal cells awakening

A day devoted to creativity, beauty and conscious creation. Through making, storytelling and sisterhood you discover that nothing is wasted. Even the broken pieces can become art.

Tuesday 30 March

The Rapture

Opening the wings

You claim your sacred yes and your sacred no. A blessing for the woman you have become, then the closing Unmasked Ball on a private wild island.

Wednesday 31 March

You return to the world as the woman who emerged.

Woven through every day

Sunrise game drives, boat cruises, barefoot bush walks, yoga at Horseshoe Bend, and a visit to the Sijwa Project. Some rituals stay inside the retreat.

Your guides

Two women, one circle

One is younger, devoted to energy work, ritual and rebirth. One is older, rooted in the earth, the animals and the initiation of menopause. Every season of womanhood has a place here.

Priestess

Rôuxvé

Once a management consultant who could solve any problem on a spreadsheet and still felt something quietly starving underneath. Her own unravelling led her into years of shamanism and tantra. Her work weaves energy healing, feminine embodiment, nervous system wisdom and ritual. She now guides women through the passage that remade her.

Keeper of the Land

Tinolla Rodgers

A hotelier for more than thirty years, founder of African Monarch Journeys, visionary behind the Sijwa Project, and a certified ThetaHealing® Practitioner. Five months into her marriage, her husband was diagnosed with two cancers. After his passing she led the lodges alone, through doubt, exhaustion and menopause. This land is where she reclaimed herself, and why Wild Woman exists.

Pain can become a portal. Endings can become beginnings. We know, because we have lived it.
